🚨 2. Spam Mention Protection & 1-Hour Cooldown

Tables: guild_settings, spam_cooldowns
/spam mention state:[on | off]
Admin

Turns the 3x Spam Mention detector ON or OFF for the server.

/spam channel channel:[#channel]
Admin

Adds or removes a specific channel from spam mention monitoring. (Note: If no channels are added, the bot automatically protects all channels when `/spam mention on` is active).

/spam log channel:[#log-channel]
Admin

Sets the log channel where red warning embeds are posted whenever someone triggers a spam mention cooldown.

⚙️ How It Works & Persistence: If User A mentions User B 3 or more times in a single message (or across rapid messages within 15 seconds), the bot deletes the message, locks User A on a 1-hour cooldown specifically from mentioning User B (saved in spam_cooldowns with an exact UTC expiration timestamp), warns User A in chat, and sends a full report to the log channel. Any attempt by User A to mention User B during that 1 hour is immediately deleted.

🎲 4. Bangla Truth or Dare System (সত্য নাকি সাহস)

Tables: tord_channels, tord_sessions
/tord channel:[#channel] action:[start | stop]
Interactive Game

Starts or stops the interactive Bangla Truth or Dare panel in the target channel.

🔄 Complete Button & Modal Workflow:
1. Choose: A player clicks Truth, Dare, or Random.
2. Ask Question (প্রশ্ন করুন): Other members see who chose what along with two buttons: 📝 প্রশ্ন করুন and 🕵️ নাম/পরিচয় গোপন রাখুন (Anonymous toggle). (The player cannot ask themselves a question!)
3. Question Modal & Media Placeholder: The asker types their question, optionally pastes a Photo/GIF link in the special media placeholder field, and chooses whether to hide their identity.
4. BINGO Button: The bot posts the question, tags the player, and displays the green BINGO button. Only that specific player can click BINGO to submit their answer and proof media/GIF.
5. Infinite Loop: Once answered, the embed updates to 🎯 খেলা সম্পন্ন! and attaches fresh Truth | Dare | Random buttons at the bottom!

🎂 8. Birthday Wishing & Smart Channel History Sync

Tables: guild_settings, user_birthdays
/birthday channel:[#channel] title:[Text] desc:[Text] url:[Media/GIF URL]
Admin

Enables automatic daily birthday wishing in #channel (checked at midnight Bangladesh Time). If you only pass channel, it uses the default Bangla birthday title, description, and celebration GIF. Any user who types their birthday in this channel automatically gets a ✅ reaction and is saved to the database.

/sync birthday_channel:[#channel]
Admin

Scans the entire message history of #birthday_channel, parses every user's birthday, adds a ✅ reaction, and saves them to Supabase.

🧠 Smart Date Parsing & Duplicate Rule:
• If a user typed their birthday twice or multiple times in history, the bot strictly saves only the LAST (most recent) birthday they typed.
• Understands formats like Dec26, August 19, 29th march, Bangla months, and numeric dates like 08/24, 3 - 5, 3 5 (where the 1st number is ALWAYS Month and the 2nd number is ALWAYS Day).
